This app is a planning and reference tool, not a substitute for a dedicated GPS device or a real safety plan. Trail closures, hazards, and fire restrictions for this corridor haven't had a dedicated research pass yet — see the Emergency screen for the fixed facts (112, coordinates, nearest-road logic) that don't depend on that research.

No physical-performance judgments are made here for anyone in the group — the app surfaces distance, elevation, terrain, and water-gap facts and leaves the judgment calls to you.
