Water is the single most important thing this app tracks, and right now it's the least researched. The only point in the database is the spring on the Kabak–Alınca climb (36.4651, 29.1447) — taken straight from the original plan, not verified in the field, not confirmed to be flowing in October. Everything else on this leg — described in the source notes as having no shops or restaurants at all — is an open question.

**Rule of thumb until proven otherwise:** treat any natural spring or cistern as needing purification. A source "looking clean" or a hiker's blog saying it's fine is not the same as it being independently confirmed safe.

The Kabak → Alınca day (10 Oct) is the highest-priority research target: steep, short, and with the least resupply of the whole trip so far.
