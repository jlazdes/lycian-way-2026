The route's southern end runs straight through Lycian civilization territory — Patara (a major Lycian League port city, with ruins and a museum right by the camp cluster), and, a bit further afield, Xanthos and Letoon (a joint UNESCO World Heritage listing). Sidyma, Pydnai, Kalabantia, and the Delikkemer aqueduct are all on the spec's watchlist too, but none of their exact detour distances from our route have been checked yet.

Worth budgeting real time for on the sea-access days rather than treating as a drive-by.
