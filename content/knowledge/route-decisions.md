Two open questions sit at the center of this itinerary, and neither has been resolved by picking a side:

**10 Oct, Kabak → Alınca.** The Paradise Beach variant shows an AllTrails closure alert citing a possible fire-season restriction — but that alert hasn't been checked directly against OGM's own current data. The main planned route doesn't go via Paradise Beach anyway, so this is a flagged side question, not a blocker for the day itself.

**12 Oct, Yediburunlar → Bel → Patara.** The original plan says 16.7 km ending at Patara. Other research floating around suggests a different distance and a different possible endpoint — Bel, Gavurağılı, or Karadere instead of Patara proper. This app deliberately keeps both without picking a winner. Don't trust either number until it's checked against current OSM data (per Culture Routes Society, OSM — not AllTrails — is the maintained route reference).

Per the Lycian Way's own maintainers, the trail changes over time and AllTrails data can lag. Every route record here stores AllTrails as a secondary source, not the authority.
