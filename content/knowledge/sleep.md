Kabak has three camp options in the original research — Secret Garden (cheapest by review reports, ~150 TRY/pp), Kabak Famous Camping (pricier, ~600 TRY/pp), and Kabak Lost Camping (price unknown). None of these are booked or confirmed with the camps directly — prices are secondhand, from reviews.

Patara has a cluster of camps near the ancient-city entrance and near the beach finish point, but no single pick has been made yet.

Ölüdeniz (night 1) and Alınca/Yediburunlar (nights 3–4) have no accommodation recorded at all yet.
